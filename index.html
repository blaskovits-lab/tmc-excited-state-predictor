Scope and interpretation. Closed-shell, mononuclear complexes; 7–85 atoms; charge −1, 0 or +1. Predictions outside familiar chemistry need separate scrutiny. Both ensembles predict DFT-reference targets. Oscillator strengths are qualitative screening outputs; ensemble uncertainty does not guarantee accuracy. The xTB ensemble was evaluated on validation molecules. The example is a DFT geometry, even when the xTB model is selected.
Molecular bonds are inferred for display. This is not an NTO visualization. No solution-color estimate is provided.
